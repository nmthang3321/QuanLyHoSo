# Page - Staff tracking

Files: `StaffTrackingView.xaml[.cs]`, `StaffTrackingViewModel.cs`, and `StaffTrackingModels.cs`. Navigation uses the `StaffTracking` key in `ShellViewModel`; reuse the shared sidebar.

- UI contains period/department/officer/status filters, metric cards, a five-row paginated staff table, selected-staff panel, active records, role-specific notices/KPI controls, performance bars, and deadline donut.
- Officer sees only `AuthContext.CurrentDisplayName`.
- Admin sends reminders. Leader has `Thông báo`/`Nhắc nhở`/`Đặt KPI` tabs; Officer receives notices.
- Leader inbox and badge count only Admin-sent messages. Saving Leader KPI requires Yes/No confirmation and sends a separate KPI notice to each affected officer, not back to Leader.
- Notices are newest first, unpaginated, scroll inside their card, bold when unread, and open a detail popup that marks them read. Shell polls every five seconds after login and stops on sign-out.
- Leadership KPI uses separate `leadership-kpi/*` routes and `LeadershipKpiTargets`.
- The performance card is now "TOP 5 CÁN BỘ XUẤT SẮC" — visible to every role (Admin, Leader, Officer) as a public commendation board. It is a deliberately minimal top-5 chart in the same style as the Dashboard "HỒ SƠ THEO ĐỊA BÀN - TOP 5" card: each row is `name (160px) | single horizontal bar | composite score`, with the bar length proportional to the score (max 270px, min 8px). Ranking uses an objective composite so idle officers cannot top the chart through the 100% empty-sample rule: `composite = 60% on-time rate + 40% completed volume`, where completed volume is the officer's CompletedCount divided by the highest CompletedCount in the period (×100). Officers with no records at all in the period are excluded from ranking. Officers load it through the dedicated aggregate LAN route `staff/top-officers` (`AppDataService.GetTopOfficers`, computed server-side without the officer self-scope), so they see the unit-wide top 5 while the paginated table and info panel stay scoped to their own data; the call is wrapped in try/catch so an older server only leaves the card empty. `StaffPerformanceRow` now carries `OnTimeCompletedCount`/`DeadlineTrackedCount` from the service; `AppDataService.EvaluateTopOfficers` (public static, unit-tested) computes the composite and a bar tooltip shows the full breakdown (`Đúng hạn x% (a/b hồ sơ có ngày hẹn)`, `Hoàn thành n — khối lượng y%`, `Điểm đánh giá z — Tốt/Khá/Cần cải thiện`). Detail visibility is privacy-scoped: for officers (`AuthContext.IsOfficer`) the a/b sample and completed-volume lines are hidden unless the hovered bar is the officer's own row (`StaffPerformanceBarRow.ShowDetail`, set by `EvaluateTopOfficers(showAllDetails, viewerName)`); leaders and admins see all details. A `MetricInfoButton` (i) icon next to the card title explains the formula, mirroring the KPI/status info icons. Bars animate horizontally via `AnimateScaleX` (`PerformanceBar_Loaded`). Deadline donut uses `StatusStat` and `StatusDonutSegmentConverter`.
- Data comes from `AppDataService`; Officer queries are scoped. `FilterCommand`, `RefreshCommand`, and `ExportCommand` are placeholders; `SelectedStaff` defaults to the first row.
